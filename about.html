About this project

Political satire and rhetorical argument are some of the greatest American past times, and the classic “shoe on the other foot” anecdote is tried and true.

In that vein, this project aims to put the shoe on the other foot, to help us realize and remember how extraordinary and truly unacceptable our daily current events are under President Trump. Take a moment to really ask yourself for each of these outrageous things Trump has done: What would we have said if Biden did this? Or god help us…. what would Fox News have said?

We can’t let immorality and corruption become normalized.
