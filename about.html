About this project

Exercising rhetorical argument is one of the greatest American past times. And the classic “shoe on the other foot” anecdote is tried and true.

In that vein, this project aims to put the shoe on the other foot, to help us realize and remember how truly extraordinary and unacceptable our daily current events are.

Let’s not let immorality and corruption become normalized.
