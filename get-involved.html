Get Involved

Information is power. So is action. There are all kinds of ways to get more involved in your community, or politics of any level. Here are a couple ideas:

Where to start

  • Attend the next No Kings Rally — October 17.

    Find a protest near you

  • Vote Save America

    Voter registration, ballot tracking, and local election guides.

  • Vote.org

    Register to vote, check your registration status, and find your polling place.

  • 5 Calls

    Scripts and phone numbers for your representatives — the best "do something in 5 minutes" option.

  • Power the Polls

    Sign up to work the polls on election day.

  • Mobilize

    Find volunteer events near you, by zip code.

  • Run for Something

    Recruits young progressives to run for local office.

  • Indivisible

    Local organizing groups and accountability actions — also behind the No Kings protests.

  • When We All Vote

    Nonpartisan voter registration and turnout organizing.

Ballot and voting guides

  • Vote411

    League of Women Voters' nonpartisan ballot guide — enter your address to see your actual ballot.

  • BallotReady

    Explains every race down the ballot.

  • Ballotpedia

    Nonpartisan encyclopedia of candidates and races.
